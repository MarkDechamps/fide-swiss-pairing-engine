## Question

Is the Olympiad Pairing Rules (Handbook D.02, a team Swiss outside C.04 that orders scoregroups much like Lim) part of "all FIDE Swiss systems", or out of scope?

## Context

Surfaced by Dubov, Burstein, Lim, Double-Swiss and acceleration (open question 7).

## Resolution

**In scope.** The Olympiad Pairing Rules (D.02) are a Pairing System the library implements, like the C.04 systems.

The ruling rests on a broader principle the human set: *everything FIDE defines is in scope so that it is possible, but the library ships good defaults as profiles* (e.g. "a simple club Swiss tournament"). Breadth of support never becomes a burden on the common user. This is now a standing Note on the map and led to the ticket "Tournament profiles".
