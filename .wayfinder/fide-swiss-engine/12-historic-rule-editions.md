## Question

Does the library support only the 2026 editions of C.04 (in force since 1 Feb 2026), or also earlier editions (e.g. Dutch 2017), so that historic tournament files can be replayed and checked? The editions differ in PAB eligibility, Dutch float rules for unplayed rounds and colour exceptions, and the endorsed reference programs may still implement the older ones, which matters for differential testing. If both are supported, is an edition a first-class concept in the model?
