## Question

Can Dutch 2017 (C.04.3 as in force until 2026-01-31) be its own procedure object over the parts Dutch 2026 already uses (the literal candidate stream and the round-wide optimum finder), and do its differences encode as edge sums too: the PPB and CLB (collapsed last bracket), C.4 applied in the penultimate bracket, and PSD (the pairing score difference) where 2026 has [C7]? Decide the object's shape, the readings the 2017 text needs, and whether it matches JaVaFo 2.2 and bbp v5 (its Oracles) on the corpus.

## Context

Graduated from the "Dutch 2017's own procedure object" fog when the frontier emptied (Double-Swiss matches in TRF). Evidence: Readable Dutch pairing algorithm (decided: Dutch 2017 as its own procedure object over shared parts), Exact optimum finder for Dutch brackets (edge weights for 2026), Verification strategy (JaVaFo 2.2 + bbp v5 are the 2017 Oracles), Historic rule editions (2017 is a verification anchor), `docs/research/dutch-system.md`.

## Resolution

Prototyped on 2026-09-25 on branch `prototype/dutch-2017` (folder `prototypes/dutch-algorithm/`, on top of the optimum-finder prototype; commits `c978096` shared parts, `1ae17dd` the 2017 procedure, `924a572` README with the shape table, readings and run instructions). The author told this session to go ahead on the recommended options and stop only at real blockers, so the decisions below are the recommended ones, for the author to overturn.

**Answer: yes.** Dutch 2017 is its own procedure object over the parts Dutch 2026 uses, and every 2017 criterion adds up over edges. It matches bbp v5.0.1 in pairs **and colours** in all 5,952 rounds, and JaVaFo 2.2 in 5,716; every JaVaFo miss is a round where JaVaFo's choice is worse on the literal text, or where JaVaFo crashes. The 2026 path, refactored to share, still matches bbp v6 on 741/741 rounds (pairs and colours).

- **Shape.** `DutchEdition` is a composition: float rule, PAB bar, criteria list, MDP order, and a factory for its own procedure. `DutchSystem` is a thin front that calls it. No inheritance.
  - Only in 2017: `Dutch2017Procedure` (A.9: pair each bracket without C.4, looking only at the following bracket (C.7); if its downfloaters plus everyone below cannot complete the round, that bracket is the Penultimate Pairing Bracket, re-paired with C.4 and without C.7, and its downfloaters plus all lower players form the Collapsed Last Bracket, paired once to complete the round), `Dutch2017Criteria` (C.5–C.19), `Dutch2017FloatRule` (A.4.b), `MdpExchanges` (D.3 order), `PabBar.BASIC_RULES_PRE_2026`.
  - Shared with 2026: `BracketPairer` (search and candidate order), `OptimumFinder`, `RoundWideMatching`, `Transpositions`, `ResidentExchange`, `ColourAllocation`, `AbsoluteCriteria`, `Failure`/`CriteriaVector`, and the new `ColourCriteria` (2017 C.8–C.11 are 2026 [C10]–[C13] word for word).
  - One new shared mechanism, `CompletionScope`: `WHOLE_ROUND` (bracket + everyone below + PAB seat, perfect matching: every 2026 bracket, and the 2017 PPB, CLB and last bracket) and `FOLLOWING_BRACKET_ONLY` (bracket + next scoregroup; each player gets a private sink vertex so it may stay unpaired).
- **Edge sums.** C.5 per downfloater. PSD (C.6) per pair (absolute score difference) and per downfloater (its score minus the bracket's lowest score − 1 point); with C.5 equal the list length is fixed, so the existing score encoding is exact. C.7 per edge to a sink or pairing two following-bracket players. C.8–C.11 per pair. C.12–C.19 per pair and per downfloater. No [C9] counterpart. PPB/CLB only decide which bracket gets `WHOLE_ROUND`. No caps; "target unreachable" still throws and never fired.
- **Results** (two batches, each generated by both bbp v5 and JaVaFo; 1,916 rounds went through the PPB/CLB route, 1,243 in the long corpus):

  | corpus | rounds | = bbp v5 | = JaVaFo |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 10–24 players | 1,130 | 1,130 | 1,130 |
  | 30–40 | 806 | 806 | 806 |
  | 55–62 | 474 | 474 | 474 |
  | stress (forfeits, HPBs, withdrawals) | 1,421 | 1,421 | 1,421 |
  | long (14–40 players, ~¾·n rounds) | 1,917 | 1,917 | 1,681 |
  | opens (90–220) | 204 | 204 | 204 |

  - **The two Oracles disagree with each other** in exactly those 236 long-corpus rounds (4%). 209 are JaVaFo choosing strictly worse at the first differing bracket (worse PSD in 177 CLBs, 6 PPBs and 1 ordinary bracket; more downfloaters in 25 PPBs; all brackets with 4–15 moved-down players); 27 are JaVaFo checker crashes (assertion error, NullPointerException). Two alternative readings (CLB as one score; completion bracket by bracket after the PPB) fit JaVaFo worse, so this is JaVaFo not finding the optimum, not a different reading. The earlier 461/461 agreement had no [C1]-saturated rounds.
  - **Speed**: mean 3–47 ms per round up to 62 players, opens 129–453 ms; slowest 2.1 s (a 40-player round 30, 15 moved-down players vs 6 residents: C(15,6) = 5,005 D.3 sets, one reachability matching each) and 3.3 s in the opens.
- **Changes to the 2026 code so it shares** (still identical to bbp v6): the 2026 walk moved unchanged into `Dutch2026Procedure`; [C5] folds into completion only for an edition with a PAB-score criterion; the [C8] look-ahead uses generic criteria (unpaired count, then their scores); the PAB bar moved from `RoundEntry` to `PabBar`; Numbered Participants drive colour parity; `CandidateCriterion.of` and `ColourCriteria` extracted.

**Decisions (recommended options, taken under the author's go-ahead):**

1. **Shape:** Dutch 2017 = its own procedure object composed in its edition factory, with `CompletionScope` as the only new shared mechanism. Confirms Readable Dutch pairing algorithm decision 3 and Historic rule editions.
2. **Eleven fixed readings, no Interpretations** (each is what both Oracles do; cited in code and README):
   - R1 (A.9) the completion check is C.1–C.3 on the downfloaters plus everyone below, one PAB-eligible leftover allowed; downfloaters may meet each other in it.
   - R2 (A.9, C.4) the PPB is re-paired with C.4 above C.5 and without C.7; the CLB is one heterogeneous bracket (PPB downfloaters as MDPs, all lower players as residents).
   - R3 (B.1.c) M1 is the number of MDPs the optimum pairs, not the most that could be paired (text slip: in 3 rounds these differ and both Oracles pair fewer with a better PSD).
   - R4 (A.8) in the CLB each player's own score counts (one collapsed score loses 107 rounds).
   - R5 (C.7) the following bracket is paired on C.1–C.3 only and compared on fewest unpaired, then their scores highest first.
   - R6 (C.7, text gap) when the following bracket is the last, an unpaired player there who may not take the PAB weighs 3 instead of 1 (bbp's `finalBrackets`; JaVaFo agrees). In the build this becomes an edition rule, not look-ahead internals.
   - R7 (C.12–C.19) "the same float" means any float given in this round, with the matching SD.
   - R8 (A.4.b) absences and forfeit losses are downfloats.
   - R9 (C.2) the PAB and forfeit wins bar the PAB; a requested full-point bye does not.
   - R10 (E.5) colour parity uses Numbered Participants' Pairing Numbers (ADR 0006 holds for 2017 too; numbering everyone loses 31 stress rounds).
   - R11 (D.3) every set of M1 MDPs, ordered by highest different score then lowest pairing positions, pruned by the optimum finder.
   - Same D.1 example slip ("6-7-8-10-11") as in the 2026 text.
3. **Dutch 2017 Oracle gate** (refines Verification strategy, which said JaVaFo and bbp v5 "must agree with each other and with us"): **bbp v5 must match exactly, pairs and colours.** JaVaFo must match except for one Known Divergence class, "JaVaFo not optimal in a PPB/CLB", each instance justified automatically by the verdict (JaVaFo worse at C.5/C.6 at the first differing bracket); JaVaFo crashes are excluded and counted.
4. **Performance:** the ≤1 s/round at ≤64 budget from Exact optimum finder holds for 2017 too; the one 2.1 s round means the build needs the D.3 lever first: prune MDP sets by score profile/prefix before asking reachability. Further levers: a matcher mode that allows unpaired players (removes the doubled sink vertices), then the existing fixed-width weights.
